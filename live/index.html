quick answers

Which sales count?

Total LIVE GMV from eligible LIVE sessions featuring the Christmas launch products, between Nov 5 and Dec 25, 2026. Any flavour from the eligible sets counts.

What if I'm #1 but under $800?

Each place has a minimum: $800 for 1st, $600 for 2nd and $200 for 3rd. You need to hit the minimum for the place you finish in to claim that prize.

Can I win more than once?

No. Creators can receive one prize. If you place in more than one challenge, you get the reward for your highest placement.

How are prizes paid?

Not via PayPal. Payment goes through an approved method provided by P.Louise. Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes on their prize.

Can I use AI-generated content?

Fully AI-generated content without clear human involvement doesn't count. Show the real product, on camera, with you in it.

Can I submit the same content twice?

No. Duplicate submissions and repetitive or low-quality content are removed from the leaderboard.
